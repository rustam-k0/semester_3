# Pitch Impressions

Teams consisted of five to six people. Teams were still forming and recruiting additional members right up to the day of the pitch.

Each team came to the front and spent a few minutes explaining what they wanted to build, which technologies they planned to use, and other key aspects of their project. Most teams brought a short presentation of five to ten slides.

Most teams proposed fairly similar technology stacks, drawing on the technologies covered in Web Engineering 2. Unix and C# appeared less frequently, particularly in game projects.

The project topics varied widely, including studying, health, games, stress, communication, and navigation.
