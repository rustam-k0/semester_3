# Module assessment rules

Term: Wintersemester 2026/27. Updated: 2026-10-09.

This reference combines course materials with the user's explicit clarifications in the chat on 2026-10-09. User-confirmed rules are recorded as such; they have not been independently verified against a teacher document. Weights do not specify rounding or conversion from points to grades unless stated. Computergrafik Grundlagen is excluded from this summary at the user's request.
